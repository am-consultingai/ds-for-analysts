?So how good is the rule's list of 50?

Of the 50 accounts the rule picks, about 15 are really leaving

15 ÷ 50 = 0.29

This is precision@50: of the 50 accounts we call, the share that were really leaving.

This is our baseline: the number any model must beat, with the same 50 calls.

?And how many of all the leavers do those 50 calls reach?

Those 15 are only 1 in 5 of the 79 accounts that leave

15 ÷ 79 = 0.19

This is recall@50: of all the accounts that leave, the share our 50 calls reach.

Even a perfect list of 50 reaches at most 50 of the 79: recall 0.63.

✗ Testing on the training rows

Like grading an exam the student saw with its answers. A model can memorise and still score high.

✓ Testing on held-back rows

The score shows how the model will do on accounts it has never seen, which is the real job.

Rule of thumb: pick rows at random, 80% to train, 20% to test. It works when the rows don't depend on time.

?The numbers can mislead. Can Claude?

Claude can change things without telling you

Drops rows

Switches the metric

Reports accuracy, where 91% means nothing when 91% of accounts stay.

Changes the split

Uses a random split, and the score goes up for the wrong reason.

Agrees with you

Push back and it apologises and changes its answer. That proves nothing.
